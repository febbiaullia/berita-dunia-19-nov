# The World Today — 19 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026, for the
make.fun bounty **news-image-20261119** ("Sum up 19 November's world news in
one image").

## The stories in the picture

- **Three presidents meet in Shenzhen** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold the
  first-ever trilateral summit at APEC, with nuclear arms and the Iran war
  on the agenda.
- **Vice City opens its doors** — GTA VI launches worldwide after a
  13-year wait; critics score it 99 out of 100.
- **World court rules on Rohingya** — The Hague delivers its verdict today,
  seven years after The Gambia filed the case.
- **Voyager 1, one light-day away** — NASA confirms the 1977 probe is now
  16 billion miles from Earth.
- **Also today:** Iran widens Gulf attacks to Kuwait; Australia sanctions
  Israeli settlers; Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast.

## How it runs

The poster is a single 1024x1024 PNG held in this repository. The program
copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
